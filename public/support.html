💬 Help and contact

Support and Contact

Contact Gramup support for questions about tasks, rewards, referrals, withdrawals, privacy, or account access.

Task issue

Include the task title, the approximate time, and what happened when verification failed.

Reward issue

Include the reward stage or task name and the points amount shown by the app.

Withdrawal issue

Include the request status and amount. Never send a private key or seed phrase.

Privacy request

Ask about correction or deletion of account data through the Gramup Telegram bot.

Contact through Telegram

The primary support channel is the official Gramup Telegram bot. Open Gramup from the button below and use the available support or contact option.

Open Gramup in TelegramRead Terms

What to include

  • Your Telegram username or numeric Telegram ID, if you are comfortable sharing it.
  • A short description of the issue and the date/time in UTC.
  • The task, referral stage, or withdrawal request involved.
  • A screenshot with private keys, passwords, and sensitive wallet secrets hidden.

Security warning

Never share a seed phrase, private key, password, or Telegram login code. Gramup support will not ask for those secrets. Support cannot guarantee a reward or reverse a confirmed blockchain transfer.

Service information

Gramup is a Telegram task and loyalty application. Support requests are reviewed during normal operating periods. Response time may vary with volume, verification needs, and third-party service availability.
