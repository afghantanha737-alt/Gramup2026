Reward Rules

Gramup rewards users with in-app points for eligible, genuine activity. These rules explain the main reward mechanisms.

No guaranteed income: Gramup points and promotional rewards are not an investment, salary, guaranteed return, or promise of profit. Reward availability may vary by task, region, campaign, and account eligibility.

1. Ways to earn points

Tasks

Complete an available task according to its instructions. Tasks currently use Telegram membership verification. Manual proof submission is not currently enabled.

Daily check-in

Eligible users may claim one daily check-in reward. Consecutive check-ins may increase a streak bonus according to the current app settings.

Referrals

Invite a real user through your referral link. The invited user must meet the activity requirement and pass anti-fraud checks before the referral becomes active.

Leaderboard

When enabled, weekly rankings are calculated from eligible positive point activity. Advertiser-funded or promotional prizes may be configured for top ranks.

2. Task eligibility

  • A task must be active and within its deadline, if a deadline is shown.
  • Only genuine human activity counts. Multiple accounts, bots, scripts, fake engagement, and coordinated manipulation are not allowed.
  • A task may have a limited capacity, sponsor budget, or verification requirement.
  • Tasks may be paused, changed, or removed if the campaign ends or the sponsor budget is exhausted.

3. Referral reward stages

Referral stages may require 10, 20, or 50 active invited users. An active invited user is a real invited account that completes the required activity. Each stage can be claimed once after its threshold is reached. Risk flags or manual review may delay eligibility.

4. Levels and badges

Levels and badges are based on eligible points earned according to the current app configuration. A displayed level is a loyalty status inside Gramup and does not represent financial status, investment performance, or a guaranteed benefit.

5. Corrections and anti-fraud review

Gramup may remove points, reject a reward, pause a claim, or review an account when activity appears invalid, duplicated, automated, misleading, or connected to abuse. We aim to apply the same rules consistently and keep an auditable transaction history.

6. Reward changes

Reward amounts, task availability, conversion rates, stage thresholds, and leaderboard prizes may change for future activity. The applicable reward is the one shown by the app when the eligible action is completed, subject to verification.

7. Questions

For a reward question or a suspected error, contact Gramup Support and include the relevant task name, approximate time, and Telegram account ID if available.
