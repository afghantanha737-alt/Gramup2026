Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

How do I start with Gramup?

Open the official Gramup bot, launch the Mini App and follow the onboarding steps. You can start with tasks, daily rewards and the mining hub.

How do I connect Tonkeeper?

Open the wallet area inside Gramup and choose TON Connect. Select Tonkeeper or MyTonWallet, then approve the connection in your wallet app.

How do referral rewards work?

Share your personal invitation link. When eligible invited users join and earn, the applicable referral reward is credited according to Gramup’s current rules.

Where can I check payout proofs?

Visit the public payment records page to review verified payouts and available Tonviewer transaction links.

Is Gramup available outside Telegram?

Gramup is designed as a Telegram Mini App. The landing page explains the ecosystem, while the full reward experience runs inside Telegram.
