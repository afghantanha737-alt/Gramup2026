Need to know

Frequently asked.

How do I start with Gramup?

Open the official Gramup bot, launch the Mini App and begin with tasks, daily rewards or the mining hub.

How do I connect Tonkeeper?

Open the wallet area, choose TON Connect and approve the connection in Tonkeeper or MyTonWallet.

How do referral rewards work?

Share your invitation link. Eligible invited users help grow your referral rewards under the current published rules.

Where can I check payout updates?

Follow the official payouts channel at @GramUpPayouts and review verified records through the public payment page.
