🔒 Data protection

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Gramup collects, why it is used, and how users can contact us about their data.

Privacy principle: Gramup collects only information needed to operate the task, reward, referral, support, and withdrawal features.

1. Information we collect

Telegram account data

Telegram user ID, first name, last name, username, profile photo when available, and language preference.

Activity data

Points, completed tasks, daily check-ins, streaks, referrals, leaderboard activity, and reward claims.

Withdrawal data

The wallet address and withdrawal details that you voluntarily submit when requesting a withdrawal.

Security data

Fraud-prevention signals such as risk flags and an irreversible network identifier hash. Raw IP addresses are not stored for referral risk checks.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate users through Telegram and provide the Mini App.
  • Calculate points, task rewards, streaks, referrals, levels, and leaderboard results.
  • Check required Telegram channel membership and task eligibility.
  • Review withdrawals and prevent duplicate, fraudulent, or abusive activity.
  • Send operational notifications through the Gramup Telegram bot, such as task, withdrawal, and reward updates.
  • Maintain service security, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.

3. Sharing and public information

We do not sell or rent Telegram names, usernames, wallet addresses, or account data. Public payment proof may show a shortened wallet address and transaction hash for independent blockchain verification, but it does not show a user's Telegram name or username.

4. Third-party services

Gramup relies on Telegram, AdsGram (optional rewarded ads shown only after you tap Watch Ad; AdsGram may receive your Telegram user ID and device information to deliver and count ad views), MongoDB hosting, Render hosting, TON network infrastructure, and other service providers needed to operate the app. These providers process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Retention and security

We retain account and transaction records as needed to operate the service, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and maintain financial history. We use access controls and technical safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You may ask about your data, request correction, or request account-data deletion by contacting support through the Support and Contact page. Some records may need to be retained for fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

7. Updates

This policy may be updated when the service or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
