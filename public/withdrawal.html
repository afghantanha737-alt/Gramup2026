Withdrawal Rules

These rules explain how an eligible Gramup user can request a conversion or withdrawal to a TON wallet.

Availability: Conversion and withdrawal are subject to current app settings, minimum thresholds, verification, network availability, regional restrictions, and available funds. A withdrawal is not guaranteed merely because a balance is displayed.

1. Eligibility

To request a withdrawal, your account must be eligible under the current Gramup rules, have enough available balance to meet the minimum threshold, and pass any required security or anti-fraud checks. Suspicious, duplicated, or disputed rewards may be placed on hold.

2. Conversion and minimum amount

Points may be converted to GRAM using the rate shown in the app. The rate and minimum withdrawal amount may change for future requests. The exact amount, token, network, and minimum requirement are shown in the Wallet before submission.

3. Wallet address responsibility

You are responsible for entering a correct wallet address and selecting a compatible network. Gramup is not responsible for assets sent to an incorrect, unsupported, or incompatible address. Never submit a private key, seed phrase, or password.

4. Review and processing

  1. Submit the withdrawal request from the Wallet.
  2. The request is recorded with a pending status.
  3. An administrator reviews eligibility, balance, destination, and transaction details.
  4. The request may be approved, rejected, cancelled, or moved to processing.
  5. After a successful transfer, the transaction hash may appear on the public payment-proof page.

5. Timing, fees, and network conditions

Processing time can vary because of manual review, service availability, network congestion, verification, and operational limits. Network or service fees may apply where disclosed. Gramup cannot guarantee a blockchain confirmation time.

6. Rejections and cancellations

A request may be rejected or cancelled if the threshold is not met, the wallet is invalid, activity fails verification, the account violates the Terms, the campaign or balance is unavailable, or technical and legal restrictions apply. The reason may be shown in the request timeline.

7. Public proof and privacy

Completed transfers may be listed on the public payment-proof page. For privacy, the page can show a shortened wallet address and transaction hash without displaying the user's Telegram name or username. See the Privacy Policy.

8. Support

If a request appears delayed or incorrect, contact Support with the request status and transaction details. Do not send private keys or recovery phrases to support.
